    When can I paint?

    The question everyone asks. Fresh plaster goes from dark pink to pale as it dries. Paint too soon and it peels.

    1. We finish

      Day 0

      Dark, glossy and cool to the touch. Leave the windows on the latch.

    2. Patchy

      Day 1 to 2

      Light patches spread from the middle. Don’t point a heater at it.

    3. Nearly there

      Day 3 to 5

      Dark corners remain. Those are the last to dry.

    4. Dry

      About a week

      Even pale pink all over. Now it’s ready for a mist coat.

    5. Paint

      The day after

      Watered-down first coat, then two full coats of your colour.
