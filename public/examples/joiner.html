Three joiners, one workshop, no flat-pack.

Thornfield is a small workshop on the Team Valley. We draw every piece for the room it will live in, cut it from boards we choose ourselves, and fit it with the same hands that made it.

That means we take on about thirty projects a year. Each one gets a named joiner from the first visit to the last screw.

From first visit to final fit

  1. We visit and measure

    An hour at your home, free. We listen, measure twice and leave you with timber samples.

  2. You get drawings and a price

    Scale drawings and one fixed price within ten days. Nothing is made until you’ve signed them off.

  3. We make it in the workshop

    You’re welcome to come and see your piece being built. Most people do.

  4. We fit, finish and tidy

    Fitted by the joiner who made it, usually in two to four days, with floors and furniture protected.
